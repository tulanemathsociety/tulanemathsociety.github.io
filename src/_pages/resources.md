## Coursework

- **[Tulane Department of Mathematics](https://sse.tulane.edu/math)** <br>
  Official department page for courses, major requirements, advising, and departmental news.

## Proofs

- **[How to Prove It](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/how-to-prove-it/6D2965D6130A35D2C8D8CF457C75CE6B)** <br>
  Daniel Velleman's standard introduction to proof: logic, quantifiers, induction, relations,
  and mathematical writing.

## LaTeX

- **[Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/)** <br>
  Online LaTeX editor for writing problem sets and notes in the browser.
- **[MacTeX for macOS](https://www.tug.org/mactex/)** <br>
  Local LaTeX distribution for macOS. Terminal install: `brew install --cask mactex-no-gui`.
- **[MiKTeX for Windows](https://miktex.org/download)** <br>
  Local LaTeX distribution for Windows. Terminal install: `winget install MiKTeX.MiKTeX`.
- **[TeX Live for Linux](https://www.tug.org/texlive/)** <br>
  Standard LaTeX distribution for Linux. On Debian or Ubuntu, a common terminal install is
  `sudo apt install texlive-full`.

## Putnam

- **[Putnam Archive](https://kskedlaya.org/putnam-archive/)** <br>
  Problems and solutions from past Putnam competitions, maintained by Kiran Kedlaya.
- **[Official Putnam information (MAA)](https://maa.org/student-programs/putnam-competition/)** <br>
  Competition overview, institutional participation details, and current announcements.
- **[Putnam and Beyond](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-387-68452-5)** <br>
  A standard text for students who want a sustained collection of advanced competition
  problems and solutions.

## Research

- **[REU Search (NSF)](https://www.nsf.gov/crssprgm/reu/list_result.jsp?unitid=5044)** <br>
  Official NSF directory of Research Experiences for Undergraduates in mathematics and
  related fields.
- **[MathPrograms.org](https://www.mathprograms.org/)** <br>
  Application portal used by many mathematics REUs, summer schools, and related programs.
- **[Alfred H. Clifford Mathematics Research Library](https://library.tulane.edu/places/Alfred-H-Clifford-Mathematics-Research-Library)** <br>
  Once it reopens, this is an excellent place to study and browse mathematics literature on
  campus.

## Grad School

- **[AMS undergraduate and graduate study resources](https://www.ams.org/programs/students/undergrad/undergrad)** <br>
  American Mathematical Society guidance on undergraduate opportunities, graduate study, and
  early career planning.

## Careers

- **[AMS career resources](https://www.ams.org/profession/career-info/career-index)** <br>
  Profiles and guidance on careers for students trained in mathematics.

## Books

- **[Abstract Algebra](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Abstract+Algebra,+3rd+Edition-p-9780471433347)** <br>
  Dummit and Foote's standard undergraduate algebra text on groups, rings, fields, and Galois
  theory.
- **[Introduction to Real Analysis](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/search?pq=Introduction+to+Real+Analysis+Bartle+Sherbert)** <br>
  Bartle and Sherbert's rigorous introduction to limits, continuity, differentiation,
  integration, and sequences of functions.
- **[Book of Proof](https://richardhammack.github.io/BookOfProof/)** <br>
  Richard Hammack's free proof text, useful alongside a first formal course in proof writing.
